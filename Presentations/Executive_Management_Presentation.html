Operational Need

The Operational Challenge in Mission-Critical Facilities

Why conventional commercial screen recording and meeting tools cannot meet enterprise defense requirements.

❌ Heavy Client Overhead

Commercial recording software consumes 20% to 50% of the CPU, degrading operator responsiveness during intense mission operations and causing workstations to freeze or stutter.

❌ Air-Gap Security Failures

Cloud-based platforms (Zoom, Teams, SaaS tools) demand continuous internet access and transmit sensitive operations data to third-party cloud infrastructure, violating strict security policies.

❌ Lack of Synchronized Replay

When an incident involves multiple operators working together, reviewing unaligned, separate video files makes it impossible to reconstruct who did what and in what exact sequence.

Workstation Integrity

Zero Workstation Impact: Hardware-Accelerated Performance

Ensuring critical mission software runs at peak speed while video is captured in real-time.

🚀 Automatic Hardware Offloading

The agent features an intelligent hardware probe. At startup, it evaluates the workstation and directs video compression straight to the graphics processor:

  • NVIDIA NVENC Acceleration: Leaves the main workstation CPU completely free (<1% usage).
  • Intel QuickSync (QSV): Achieves ~37% less CPU usage than traditional software recorders on standard desktop workstations.
  • No User Configuration Needed: Adapts automatically across mixed fleets of laptops, standard desktops, and high-end workstations.

Operational Reliability

24/7 High Availability & Offline Resiliency

Designed to ensure zero gap in critical security audits even during network cuts or storage outages.

🔌 Network Disconnect Protection

If the network link to the server drops, the endpoint agent automatically switches to local encrypted caching. When connectivity restores, recorded video is seamlessly synchronized.

💾 Dual Storage Safeguard

Primary recordings write directly to enterprise NetApp network storage. If NetApp becomes unreachable, the server auto-diverts recordings to local high-speed disk within 3 seconds.

🔄 Self-Healing Processes

Server supervisor workers continuously monitor core streaming processes. In the event of an abnormal crash, services auto-recover within 5 seconds without requiring IT intervention.

Forensic Integrity

The "Time-Continuity Guarantee": Gap-Proof Audits

Overcoming station reboots and power outages without corrupting multi-station alignment.

❓ The Risk with Traditional Videos

If Operator A worked continuously, but Operator B restarted their workstation for 5 minutes, traditional video players collapse the missing 5 minutes. The videos immediately desynchronize, rendering investigation recordings misleading or legally inadmissible.

🛡️ The ITB "No-Signal" Guarantee

ITB-SCREEN-RECORDER automatically injects standardized "No-Signal" frames for any offline duration.

  • Total time alignment is preserved across all tracks.
  • Clear visual distinction between active recording and power-down periods.
  • Ready for certified courtroom, military debrief, or compliance reviews.
Detailed Audit

Forensic Keystroke Capture & Synchronized Subtitles

Correlating operator intent with screen outcomes for definitive audit trails.

⌨️ Input Verification

Records key combinations (shortcuts, command executions, system triggers) with exact millisecond timestamps, proving what commands were entered.

📄 Synchronized Subtitles

Exported videos can include automatic SubRip (.SRT) subtitles. Keystrokes appear overlaid at the bottom of the video exactly when they occurred on screen.

🔒 Privacy Protection Gate

Keystroke logging is strictly bound to active recording. If a station is logged off or in standby, keyboard capture is automatically shut down.

Infrastructure Efficiency

Enterprise Storage Economics & Intelligent Retention

Predictable storage consumption with automated lifecycle policies.

📦 Deterministic 5-Minute Chunks
  • Recordings are segmented precisely every 5 minutes on the clock (e.g., 10:00, 10:05).
  • Eliminates massive, vulnerable 10-hour single video files that can corrupt easily.
  • Enables immediate parallel extraction of any 10-minute incident without scanning days of data.
🧹 Automated Retention & Safety Shield
  • Automated Purge: Files older than the configured policy (e.g. 30 days) are automatically deleted without human intervention.
  • Active Recording Shield: Live sessions are protected from accidental premature deletion during high-capacity events.
  • High Compression Ratio: Standardized H.264 profiles provide crisp 1080p legibility with minimal network bandwidth usage.
